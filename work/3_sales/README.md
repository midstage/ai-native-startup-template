# 3_sales: sales

- **Produces:** contracts.
- **Receives:** demand and leads from marketing (`2_demand`).
- **Feeds:** customer success (`4_success`).
- **Put here:** the sales process, offers and pricing, proposal and contract templates, objection handling.
- **Not here:** anything about one specific prospect. That goes in their folder in `8_companies/`.
- **Agents here** draft. Anything that reaches a prospect is a one-way door: the founder approves and sends it.
