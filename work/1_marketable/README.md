# 1_marketable: product and engineering

- **Produces:** marketable features. Not features in general: features someone will pay for and marketing can talk about.
- **Receives:** capacity from back office, people and agents (`6_capacity`).
- **Feeds:** marketing (`2_demand`).
- **Put here:** product specs, roadmap, research on what to build, content and IP that exist independent of any one customer.
- **Agents here** should know their ultimate goal is more marketable features, not more code.
