# Flywheel

How this business turns its work into growth. Each segment produces one output, and that output is the input of the next segment. Agents read this file to know which segment they serve and what "more" means there.

The flywheel interview fills in this file. Anything marked TBD is still open, and Unknown means nobody knows yet. Never fill a segment with guesses.

## The loop

1. **Product and engineering** (`work/1_marketable`) produces **marketable features**.
2. **Marketing** (`work/2_demand`) turns them into **demand and leads**.
3. **Sales** (`work/3_sales`) turns leads into **contracts**.
4. **Customer success** (`work/4_success`) turns contracts into **onboarded, happy, paying customers**.
5. **Finance** (`work/5_runway`) turns revenue into **runway**, and reinvests it.
6. **Back office, people and agents** (`work/6_capacity`) turn runway into **capacity**: more people or more agents, and better systems.
7. More capacity builds more marketable features, and the loop spins again.
