# Priorities review

A business grows only as fast as its weakest flywheel segment. More work on a strong segment doesn't help while a weak one holds the loop back. Founders, technical ones especially, tend to keep building product while the rest of the flywheel stalls. This review finds the constraint and points the week at it.

## Start fast

Read `flywheel.md`, last week's goals in it, `strategy.md` (this quarter's priorities and critical number), and `backlog.md` (what got done, what's stuck, what's Waiting for human).

Ask one question, all at once:

**"For each of the six segments, red, yellow or green? And what changed since last week?"**

Write the colors into the Health lines of `flywheel.md`. Go into a longer conversation only for a segment that is red or unclear.

## Find the constraint

1. Pick the one segment that most holds the loop back. Base it on evidence: a number, a stuck job, something the founder said. Not on how busy the segment feels.
2. Name it under **The constraint** in `flywheel.md`, with the evidence.
3. Say it in one line and ask the founder to confirm or correct it: "The constraint this week looks like <segment>, because <evidence>. Agree?"

## Set the week

1. Review last week's goals: done, partly done, or not done, and why, in one line each.
2. Propose one to three goals for this week. At least one should strengthen the constraint, and every goal should move a priority for this quarter in `strategy.md`. Each goal says what will be true at the end of the week.
3. Write the confirmed goals under **Goals this week** in `flywheel.md`. Anything that lasts longer than a week is a quarterly priority: propose adding it to `strategy.md` and ask first.

## Clean the backlog

1. Re-link the Ready and New jobs in `backlog.md` to this week's goals (essential, important, nice to have).
2. List the jobs that now serve no goal. Propose closing them as one batch: "These serve none of this week's goals. I'd close them all. Which do you want to keep?" Close only what the founder confirms.

## Close

- Record any real change of direction in `docs/decisions/`.
- Commit `flywheel.md` and `backlog.md`.
- Report in five lines or fewer: the constraint, this week's goals, and how many jobs were closed.

Plain English, numbered items, and judgment calls only. Settle details yourself.
